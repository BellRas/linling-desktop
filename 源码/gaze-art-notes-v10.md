# 16 向整身视线素材

`gaze-full-pose-sheet-key-v10.png` 使用 Codex 内置 imagegen 生成。造型参考为 `idle-key-v06.png`，方向顺序参考为 `预览/gaze-16-v09.png`；后者的肩颈拼接是需要修正的问题，不作为成品模型。最终素材由 `extract_gaze_v10.py` 从原图连通区域提取 16 个完整角色，使用共同缩放比例、脚底定位、原图集色板和硬边透明度生成 `gaze-full-v10.png`。`build_character_v05.py` 将完整姿势写入主图集并生成呼吸视线图集。

最终采用的绘图提示词：

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production pixel-art 16-direction gaze animation sprite sheet for existing desktop pet Linling. Image 1 is the authoritative full-body character model. Image 2 is the direction order guide but its neck/shoulder seams are WRONG; redraw every character as a complete coherent full-body pose, never paste a head onto a fixed torso. Draw EXACTLY 16 separate complete full-body pixel-art sprites in a strict 4-column by 4-row grid, one sprite centered inside each equal cell, transparent gutters and no text or borders. Direction order row-major: 00 look up, 01 up slightly right, 02 up-right, 03 right slightly up; 04 right, 05 right slightly down, 06 down-right, 07 down slightly right; 08 down, 09 down slightly left, 10 down-left, 11 left slightly down; 12 left, 13 left slightly up, 14 up-left, 15 up slightly left. Head angle, face, neck, shoulders and upper body should turn or tilt naturally and subtly; torso, arms, bell, robe hem, tail and legs form one coherent figure in every direction. All 16 maintain the exact same height, head-to-body ratio, shoulder width, exposed leg length, foot line, hat, bow, tail, ornate sage-cream robe and bell, palette and black pixel outlines as image 1. All foot anchors stay at same y coordinate. Only direction changes; expression calm and adorable. Adjacent sprites change smoothly with no flashes in scale or textures. Hard crisp nearest-neighbor pixel edges and true transparent background, no anti-aliasing, shadows, captions, extra characters or objects. Character must fit fully within each grid cell, with generous transparent separation. Avoid bent/twisted anatomy, duplicated limbs, truncated hair/tail. This is a sprite sheet, not a single illustration.

生成模型并不能严格保证格线或原色板，所以发布素材由脚本处理，并由 `verify_gaze_v10.py` 检查。动画的动态观感还需结合 `预览/gaze-sweep-v10.gif` 和实际桌宠运行确认。
